What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?
The fastest-growing role in enterprise AI — bridging the gap between AI research and real-world deployment.

An FDE is an AI engineer who works directly with enterprise clients to deploy production-grade AI systems — handling everything from business translation to database security to cloud deployment.
